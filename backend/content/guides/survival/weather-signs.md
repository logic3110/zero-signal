## Steps
1. Towering cumulus clouds building upward in the afternoon often become thunderstorms; plan to be off exposed ridges by early afternoon.
2. High thin cirrus clouds followed by thickening, lowering cloud often mean rain or snow within 12-24 hours.
3. A sudden drop in temperature, gusty wind shift, or dark anvil-shaped clouds mean a storm is close.
4. If your phone or watch has a barometer, a fast pressure drop means worsening weather.
5. In valleys, listen for thunder; if you hear it, you are within range of lightning.
6. Set a turn-back time before you start and keep to it if weather deteriorates.

## Warnings
- Do not cross streams during or after heavy rain; flash floods rise quickly.
- Do not shelter under isolated trees during a thunderstorm.
