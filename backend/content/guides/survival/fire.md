## Steps
1. Pick a sheltered spot away from overhanging branches, dry grass and tents. Clear a circle down to bare soil.
2. Collect three types of dry material: tinder (dry grass, bark shavings, cotton, paper), kindling (pencil-thick sticks), and fuel (wrist-thick and larger wood).
3. Make a small loose pile of tinder and lean kindling around it in a tepee shape, leaving a gap on the windward side.
4. Light the tinder from the windward side and blow gently at the base.
5. Add bigger kindling gradually as it catches, then fuel wood.
6. Keep a supply of dry wood under cover and keep the fire small; it wastes less fuel.
7. To put it out, drown it with water, stir the ashes and drown again until cold to the touch.

## Warnings
- Do not light fires during a fire ban or in dry, windy conditions.
- Do not burn fires inside a closed tent or shelter; carbon monoxide can kill.
- Do not use petrol to start a fire.
