## Steps
1. Start preparing at least 2 hours before dark.
2. Choose a sheltered spot out of the wind, near your last known position and visible to searchers.
3. Put on dry layers and a hat before you get cold.
4. Build insulation under you with leaves, branches or your pack, and a windbreak or shelter over you.
5. Collect three times as much firewood as you think you need, if a fire is safe.
6. Gather water now and keep it where it will not freeze (inside your shelter).
7. Set out a torch, whistle and phone within reach, and keep your phone warm in an inner pocket.
8. Eat something before sleeping and wiggle fingers and toes to stay warm.

## Warnings
- Do not sleep in wet clothes if you can avoid it.
- Do not drink alcohol to "warm up".
