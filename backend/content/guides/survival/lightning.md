## Steps
1. If you can hear thunder, you are close enough to be struck. Go into a substantial building or a hard-topped vehicle with windows closed.
2. Outdoors, get off peaks, ridges and open fields immediately, and move to lower ground.
3. Stay away from isolated tall trees, poles, metal fences and water.
4. In a group, spread out at least 15 metres apart so one strike does not injure everyone.
5. If you have no shelter, crouch on insulating material (a foam pad or pack) with feet together, head tucked, and hands over ears.
6. Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder before going back out.
7. Lightning strike victims do not carry a charge and are safe to touch. Start CPR if they are not breathing normally.

## Warnings
- Do not shelter under a lone tree or in a tent in open ground.
- Do not lie flat on the ground.

## When to get help
- Anyone struck by lightning needs emergency medical care.
