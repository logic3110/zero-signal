## Steps
1. DROP to your hands and knees wherever you are.
2. COVER your head and neck under a sturdy table or desk, or against an inside wall away from windows.
3. HOLD ON until the shaking stops.
4. If in bed, stay there and protect your head with a pillow.
5. If outdoors, move to an open area away from buildings, trees, walls and power lines, then drop.
6. If driving, stop in a clear place away from bridges and overpasses and stay in the vehicle.
7. After shaking stops, check yourself and others for injuries. Leave damaged buildings carefully using the stairs.
8. Smell for gas; if you smell it, open windows, leave, and do not use switches or flames.
9. Expect aftershocks and stay out of damaged buildings.

## Warnings
- Do not run outside during shaking; falling debris causes many injuries.
- Do not use lifts.
- Do not stand in doorways in modern buildings; they are not stronger than other areas.

## When to get help
- If trapped, tap on pipes or walls or use a whistle rather than shouting, to save energy and avoid inhaling dust.
