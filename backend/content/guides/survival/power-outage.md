## Steps
1. Use torches or battery lanterns instead of candles to reduce fire risk.
2. Keep the fridge and freezer doors closed as much as possible.
3. Unplug sensitive appliances to protect them from surges when power returns.
4. Keep phones charged with power banks or a car charger; switch on battery saver.
5. If someone depends on powered medical equipment or refrigerated medicines like insulin, plan for backup power or a place to go.
6. Place generators outdoors, at least 6 metres from doors and windows.
7. In hot weather, follow heatwave precautions; in cold weather, dress in layers and gather in one room.

## Warnings
- Do not use generators, charcoal or gas stoves indoors or in garages.
- Do not open the freezer to check food repeatedly.
