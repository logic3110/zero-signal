## Steps
1. Follow evacuation orders and leave early. Do not wait to see the flames.
2. Move away from the fire at right angles to the wind direction, or towards areas already burnt or with little fuel (rock, roads, water, ploughed fields).
3. Fire moves fastest uphill; do not flee uphill ahead of a fire.
4. Cover your nose and mouth with a damp cloth and keep low to avoid smoke.
5. If trapped in a vehicle, park away from vegetation, keep the engine running, close windows and vents, and get down below the window level under a blanket.
6. If trapped on foot, lie face down in a ditch or depression and cover yourself with soil or a non-synthetic blanket.

## Warnings
- Do not try to outrun a fire uphill.
- Do not shelter in water tanks or wells.
