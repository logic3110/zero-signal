## Steps
1. During heavy rain in hills, listen for rumbling sounds, cracking trees, or boulders knocking together.
2. Watch for new cracks in the ground or walls, tilting trees or poles, and sudden muddy water in streams.
3. If you see or hear signs, move quickly away from the path of the slide, sideways out of the valley or to higher stable ground.
4. If escape is not possible, curl into a tight ball and protect your head.
5. When driving on hill roads in rain, watch for fallen rocks and collapsed road edges; do not stop below steep slopes.
6. After a slide, stay away from the area; more slides may follow.

## Warnings
- Do not cross a landslide-damaged road or bridge.
- Do not shelter below steep slopes or in valley bottoms during heavy rain.
