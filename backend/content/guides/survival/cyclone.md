## Steps
1. Follow official forecasts and evacuate from coastal and low-lying areas if told to; storm surge is the biggest killer.
2. Before the storm, secure or bring inside loose objects, charge phones and power banks, and store drinking water and food for several days.
3. Shelter in a strong building. Stay in a small inner room or corridor on the lowest floor that will not flood, away from windows.
4. Switch off electricity and gas if instructed.
5. If the wind suddenly drops, you may be in the eye of the cyclone; stay inside because the wind will return from the other direction.
6. After the storm, watch for fallen power lines, damaged buildings and flood water.

## Warnings
- Do not go outside during the storm or in the calm eye.
- Do not use generators or charcoal stoves indoors; carbon monoxide can kill.
