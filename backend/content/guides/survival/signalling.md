## Steps
1. Stay in an open, visible place near where you were last known to be.
2. Use signals in threes: three whistle blasts, three fires in a triangle, three flashes, repeated regularly.
3. Use a signal mirror or phone screen to flash sunlight at aircraft or distant people: aim through your fingers in a V.
4. Lay out bright clothing, a tarp, or rocks in a large X or SOS on open ground, at least 3 metres tall.
5. At night, use a torch or the SOS flashlight mode: three short, three long, three short.
6. Make smoke in daylight by adding green leaves to a fire, and keep fires bright at night.
7. Try an SMS with your coordinates; texts can get through when calls cannot. Move to higher ground to find signal.

## Warnings
- Do not waste your phone battery; switch on airplane mode between attempts.
- Do not leave a signal fire unattended.
