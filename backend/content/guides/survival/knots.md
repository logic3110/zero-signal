## Steps
1. Reef (square) knot for joining two ends of equal thickness, e.g. bandages: right over left and under, then left over right and under.
2. Bowline for a loop that will not slip: make a small loop in the standing part, pass the end up through it, around behind the standing part, and back down through the loop. Tighten.
3. Clove hitch to attach a rope to a pole: wrap around the pole, cross over the first turn, wrap again and tuck the end under the second turn.
4. Taut-line hitch for adjustable tension on tent lines: pass the end around the anchor, make two turns inside the loop, then one turn outside and tighten; slide it to adjust.
5. Always test a knot with your weight or load before relying on it.

## Warnings
- Do not use a reef knot for climbing or loads; it can slip.
