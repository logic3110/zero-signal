## Steps
1. Look for flowing water from streams or springs, collect rain on plastic sheets, or collect dew with a cloth in the early morning.
2. Let cloudy water settle, then pour it through a clean cloth or coffee filter to remove particles.
3. Boil: bring water to a rolling boil for 1 minute. Above 2,000 m altitude, boil for 3 minutes. Let it cool covered.
4. If you cannot boil, use unscented household bleach (5-6%): 2 drops per litre of clear water, or 4 drops per litre if cloudy or very cold. Stir and wait at least 30 minutes; there should be a slight chlorine smell.
5. Water purification tablets: follow the packet instructions exactly and wait the full contact time.
6. Solar disinfection (SODIS): fill clear plastic bottles with clear water and lay them in full sun for 6 hours, or 2 days if cloudy.
7. Store treated water in clean, covered containers.

## Warnings
- Do not drink seawater or urine.
- Do not drink flood water or water near mines, factories or farms with chemicals; boiling does not remove chemicals.
- Do not eat snow to hydrate; melt it first to avoid lowering body temperature.

## When to get help
- Severe diarrhoea, vomiting or fever after drinking untreated water; see the dehydration guide.
