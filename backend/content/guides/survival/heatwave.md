## Steps
1. Drink water regularly even if you are not thirsty. Use ORS or lemon water with a pinch of salt if sweating heavily.
2. Stay indoors or in shade between about 12 noon and 3 pm.
3. Wear light, loose, light-coloured cotton clothing and cover your head.
4. Keep your home cool: close curtains during the day, open windows at night, and use wet cloths and fans.
5. Take cool showers or wipe the skin with a wet cloth.
6. Check on elderly neighbours, young children, pregnant women and people with chronic illness at least twice a day.
7. Learn the signs of heat exhaustion and heatstroke; see the heat illness guide.

## Warnings
- Do not leave children or pets in parked vehicles.
- Avoid alcohol, tea, coffee and heavy meals in the heat.
- Avoid strenuous outdoor activity in the afternoon.
