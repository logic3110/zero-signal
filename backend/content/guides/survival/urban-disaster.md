## Steps
1. Decide quickly: shelter in place (for chemical releases, severe storms) or evacuate (fire, flood, structural damage) according to official advice.
2. To shelter in place from a chemical release, go indoors, close windows and doors, turn off fans and air conditioning, and seal gaps with wet towels or tape.
3. Keep a battery or hand-crank radio for official updates when mobile networks are down.
4. Use stairs, not lifts. Know two ways out of your building.
5. If you smell gas, leave immediately without using switches, lighters or phones inside.
6. Check on neighbours, especially older people and those living alone.

## Warnings
- Do not enter damaged buildings.
- Do not spread unverified rumours; rely on official channels.
