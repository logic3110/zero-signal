## Steps
1. Choose a site on high, dry ground, out of the wind, away from dead trees, rockfall, and dry stream beds that could flood.
2. Use what exists first: a vehicle, rock overhang, dense trees, or the lee of a boulder.
3. Insulate from the ground with a thick layer of dry leaves, branches, a pack or foam. You lose more heat to the ground than to the air.
4. Build a simple lean-to or A-frame with a ridge pole and branches, covered with a tarp, plastic or layers of leaves and grass.
5. Keep it small, just big enough to lie in; it will stay warmer.
6. Put on all dry layers, cover your head, and share body heat with companions.
7. In the heat, build shade instead and rest during the hottest hours.

## Warnings
- Do not shelter in dry river beds or narrow gullies during rain.
- Do not sleep directly on cold ground or snow without insulation.
