## Steps
1. Agree on a family meeting point near home and one outside your area, and an out-of-area contact person.
2. Keep a grab bag ready: water, non-perishable food, medicines with a list, first-aid kit, torch, batteries, power bank, copies of documents in a waterproof bag, cash, whistle, clothes and a blanket.
3. Include items for babies, elderly people, pets and anyone with a disability.
4. When told to evacuate, leave immediately. Lock your home and follow the recommended routes.
5. Tell your contact person where you are going.
6. Keep the supplies tracker in ZeroSignal updated so you know what is running low.

## Warnings
- Do not take shortcuts; they may be blocked or dangerous.
- Do not return until authorities say it is safe.
