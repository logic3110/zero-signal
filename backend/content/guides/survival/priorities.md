## Steps
1. Stop and calm down. Sit, drink a little water, and think before acting.
2. Deal with immediate danger (fire, water, falling rock, traffic) and serious injuries first.
3. Remember the rule of 3s: about 3 minutes without air, 3 hours without shelter in harsh weather, 3 days without water, 3 weeks without food.
4. Protect yourself from cold, heat, wind and rain; exposure is the most common killer outdoors.
5. Find or make safe drinking water.
6. Make yourself visible and signal for help; tell people where you are if you have any signal.
7. Food is the lowest priority in the first days; conserve energy instead.
8. Take stock of what you have and ration your phone battery: switch on airplane mode and check for signal only occasionally.

## Warnings
- Do not panic-walk in the dark or in bad weather.
- Do not eat unknown plants or mushrooms.

## When to get help
- Use any signal to send your location by SMS or call the emergency number; SMS often gets through when calls fail.
