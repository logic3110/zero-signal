## Steps
1. Make noise while walking in dense vegetation so animals hear you coming.
2. If you see a large animal, stop, stay calm and give it space. Back away slowly while facing it.
3. Leopard or big cat: do not run or turn your back. Make yourself look big, keep eye contact, and back away. If attacked, fight back.
4. Bear: back away slowly and speak calmly. For a sloth or brown bear that attacks, protect your head and neck; fight back if it continues.
5. Elephant: keep a large distance and a clear escape route. If it charges, move away quickly behind large obstacles or uphill.
6. Monkeys: do not show food or make eye contact; drop food and walk away calmly.
7. Snakes: freeze, then step back slowly. Most bites happen when people try to catch or kill snakes.
8. Store food away from your shelter and keep camp clean.

## Warnings
- Do not run from predators; it can trigger a chase.
- Do not approach young animals; the mother is usually nearby.

## When to get help
- Any bite or claw wound from a wild animal needs cleaning and medical assessment for rabies and infection.
