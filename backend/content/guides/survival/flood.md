## Steps
1. Follow official warnings and evacuate early if told to. Move to higher ground immediately if water is rising.
2. Before leaving, switch off electricity at the mains and turn off gas if it is safe to do so.
3. Take your emergency kit: water, food, medicines, documents in a plastic bag, phone, charger and torch.
4. If trapped in a building, go to the highest floor or the roof (not a closed attic) and signal for help.
5. Stay away from flood water: 15 cm of moving water can knock you down and 30 cm can float a car.
6. After the flood, return only when authorities say it is safe. Wear boots and gloves when cleaning.
7. Boil or disinfect all drinking water until the supply is declared safe.

## Warnings
- Do not walk, swim or drive through flood water. Turn around, don't drown.
- Do not touch electrical equipment when wet or standing in water.
- Do not eat food that touched flood water.

## When to get help
- Call the emergency or disaster helpline if trapped, injured, or if someone is swept away.
