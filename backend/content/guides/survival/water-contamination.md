## Steps
1. Follow any boil-water advisory from local authorities.
2. Use bottled water or water you have boiled or disinfected for drinking, cooking, brushing teeth, making ice and preparing baby formula.
3. Disinfect wells that were flooded before using them again, following local health department instructions.
4. Wash hands with soap and treated water, especially before food and after the toilet.
5. Clean and disinfect storage containers before filling them.

## Warnings
- Do not use water that smells of fuel or chemicals; boiling will not make it safe.
- Do not let children play in flood water.

## When to get help
- Watery diarrhoea with vomiting in several people may be cholera; seek medical help and use ORS immediately.
