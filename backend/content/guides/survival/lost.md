## Steps
1. STOP as soon as you realise you are lost. Sit down, drink some water and calm down.
2. THINK: when did you last know where you were? Do not move without a reason.
3. OBSERVE: look for trails, streams, ridgelines, sounds of roads or people. Check your phone for GPS coordinates even without signal.
4. PLAN: if someone knows your route and expected return time, the best plan is usually to stay where you are.
5. Mark your position so you can return to it if you explore a little. Never go out of sight of it.
6. Make yourself visible and signal regularly; see the signalling guide.
7. Prepare shelter and warmth well before dark.
8. Only move if you must (danger, no water, nobody knows where you are). Then travel downhill along a stream or ridge towards known roads in daylight, marking your route.

## Warnings
- Do not keep walking in the dark or in fog.
- Do not split up a group.
- Children should "hug a tree": stay in one place and answer when called.

## When to get help
- Send an SMS with your coordinates to your emergency contacts and call the emergency number whenever you have any signal.
