## Steps
1. Eat perishable food from the fridge first, then freezer food, then non-perishable stored food.
2. A closed fridge keeps food cold for about 4 hours without power; a full freezer for about 48 hours.
3. Throw away perishable food that has been above 5 degrees C for more than 2 hours, or that smells or looks wrong.
4. Throw away any food that has touched flood water, including sealed cans that are dented or swollen.
5. In the wild, ration food and prioritise energy conservation.
6. Only eat wild plants you can identify with complete certainty.
7. Wash hands with soap and treated water before handling food.

## Warnings
- Do not eat wild mushrooms unless an expert has identified them; many deadly species look like edible ones.
- Do not taste food to check whether it has spoiled.
- "When in doubt, throw it out."
