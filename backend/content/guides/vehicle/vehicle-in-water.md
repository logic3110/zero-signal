## Steps
1. Stay calm and act immediately; you have only a short time while the car floats.
2. Seatbelts: unfasten yours.
3. Windows: open or break a side window (not the windscreen) while the electrics still work. Use a window breaker or headrest prongs at the corner of the glass.
4. Children: release them, oldest first, and push them out ahead of you.
5. Out: climb out through the window and swim to safety or climb onto the roof.
6. Once out, move away from the vehicle and call for help.

## Warnings
- Do not try to open the door while water is outside; pressure keeps it shut.
- Do not call for help first while still inside; escape first.
- Do not drive into flood water; 30 cm of moving water can float a car.
