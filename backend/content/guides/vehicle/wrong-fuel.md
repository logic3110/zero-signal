## Steps
1. If you realise at the pump that you used the wrong fuel, do not start the engine or even switch on the ignition.
2. Put the car in neutral and push it to a safe place with help.
3. Call a roadside assistance or fuel-drain service to drain and flush the tank.
4. If you realise while driving (engine misfiring, knocking, smoke, loss of power), pull over safely and switch off the engine immediately.
5. If you run out of fuel, pull over as far off the road as possible with hazard lights on.
6. Get fuel in an approved container. For diesel engines, you may need to prime the fuel system after refilling; see the owner's manual.

## Warnings
- Do not keep driving with the wrong fuel; it can destroy the fuel pump and injectors.
- Do not walk along a busy highway to get fuel; call for help instead.
