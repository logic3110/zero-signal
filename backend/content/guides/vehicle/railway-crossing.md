## Steps
1. Get everyone out of the vehicle immediately. Do not try to restart it or push it if a train may be coming.
2. Move away from the tracks at a 45-degree angle towards the direction the train is coming from, so you are not hit by flying debris.
3. Call the emergency number and give the crossing location or identification number if posted.
4. If there is a crossing keeper or signal phone, alert them.
5. Only return to the vehicle if officials confirm no trains are coming.

## Warnings
- Do not stay in the vehicle.
- Do not stop on the tracks in traffic; wait until there is space on the other side before crossing.
