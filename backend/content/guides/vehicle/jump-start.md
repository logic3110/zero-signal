## Steps
1. Check both vehicles use the same battery voltage (usually 12 V). Park them close without touching. Switch off both ignitions and all lights.
2. Check the dead battery for cracks, leaks or bulging. If damaged, do not jump-start it.
3. Connect one red clamp to the positive (+) terminal of the dead battery.
4. Connect the other red clamp to the positive (+) terminal of the good battery.
5. Connect one black clamp to the negative (-) terminal of the good battery.
6. Connect the last black clamp to an unpainted metal part of the dead car's engine block or a designated earth point, away from the battery.
7. Start the good car and let it run for 2-3 minutes, then start the dead car.
8. Once running, remove the cables in reverse order: black from the dead car, black from the good car, red from the good car, red from the dead car.
9. Drive the revived car for at least 20-30 minutes to recharge the battery.

## Warnings
- Do not let the clamps touch each other while connected.
- Do not lean over the battery; batteries can release explosive gas.
- Do not smoke or make sparks near the battery.
- Hybrid and electric vehicles have special jump points; check the manual first.

## When to get help
- If the car still does not start after a few attempts, the battery, starter or alternator may be faulty.
