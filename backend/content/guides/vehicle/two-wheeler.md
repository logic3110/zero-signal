## Steps
1. Won't start: check the kill switch is on RUN, the side stand is up (many bikes cut out with it down), there is fuel, and the fuel tap is on (or switch to reserve).
2. Kick start: switch the ignition on, find compression by gently pushing the kick lever until resistance is felt, let it return, then kick firmly through the full stroke.
3. Fouled spark plug: remove the plug cap, unscrew the plug with a plug spanner, clean black deposits with a cloth or wire brush, check the gap, refit and try again.
4. Chain came off or is loose: put the bike on the centre stand, fit the chain over the rear sprocket first then roll the wheel to seat it. Adjust tension so there is about 2-3 cm of up-and-down play.
5. Tubeless puncture: find the object, remove it, ream the hole with the kit tool, insert a sticky plug with the needle, trim it, and reinflate to the correct pressure.
6. Tube-type puncture: you usually need to remove the wheel and patch or replace the tube; push the bike slowly to a repair shop if you cannot.

## Warnings
- Do not ride with a slack or damaged chain; it can lock the rear wheel.
- Tubeless plug repairs are temporary; ride gently and get the tyre checked.
- Keep fingers away from the chain and sprocket when the engine is running.
