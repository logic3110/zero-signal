## Steps
1. Turn the key or press start and listen. Note whether the dashboard lights come on and how bright the headlights are.
2. Rapid clicking with dim or flickering lights usually means a flat or weak battery. Try a jump start.
3. A single loud click with bright lights often points to the starter motor or its connection.
4. No sound and no lights: check the battery terminals for looseness or white/green corrosion. Clean and tighten them.
5. Engine cranks normally but does not fire: check you have fuel, and that the immobiliser or key battery is working (try the spare key).
6. For a manual car with a weak battery, a push start may work: second gear, clutch down, ignition on, push to walking speed, then release the clutch quickly.
7. If a jump start works but the battery goes flat again, the alternator or battery may be failing.

## Warnings
- Do not push-start automatic, hybrid or most modern diesel cars with catalytic converters unless the manual allows it.
- Do not keep cranking for more than 10 seconds at a time; wait a minute between tries.

## When to get help
- The battery warning light stays on while driving; the alternator may not be charging.
