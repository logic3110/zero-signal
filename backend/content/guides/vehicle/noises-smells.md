## Steps
1. Squeal when starting or turning: loose or worn drive belt.
2. Grinding or metal scraping when braking: worn brake pads; get them checked immediately.
3. Knocking from the engine that gets faster with engine speed: low oil or engine damage. Stop and check the oil.
4. Clunking over bumps: worn suspension parts. Drive slowly to a mechanic.
5. Vibration through the steering wheel at speed: wheel balance or a damaged tyre. Check tyres for bulges.
6. Smell of burning rubber or plastic: slipping belt or an electrical fault. Stop and check.
7. Sweet syrupy smell: coolant leak. Smell of raw fuel: fuel leak; do not start the engine and move away if it is strong.
8. Blue smoke from the exhaust: burning oil. White thick smoke: coolant entering the engine. Black smoke: too much fuel.
9. Smoke from under the bonnet: pull over, switch off, get everyone out and follow the vehicle fire card if you see flames.

## Warnings
- Do not ignore burning smells or smoke.
- Do not open the bonnet fully if you suspect a fire.
