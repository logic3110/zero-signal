## Steps
1. Note the colour of the light. Red means a serious problem or safety issue. Amber or yellow means a fault to check soon. Green or blue are information only.
2. Red oil pressure light (oil can): stop the engine as soon as it is safe. Check the oil level when the engine has cooled for a few minutes.
3. Red temperature light (thermometer in liquid): pull over and follow the overheating guide.
4. Red battery light: the alternator may not be charging. Switch off non-essential electrics and drive to help; the car may stop when the battery runs down.
5. Red brake light: check the handbrake is off. If it stays on, brake fluid may be low; stop and get help.
6. Amber engine (check engine) light steady: drive gently and have it checked soon. Flashing: a serious misfire; reduce speed and stop driving as soon as possible to avoid damage.
7. Amber ABS, airbag, tyre pressure or traction lights: drive carefully and get them checked. For tyre pressure, check all tyres at the next safe stop.
8. See the warning lights reference in the Vehicle tools for more symbols.

## Warnings
- Do not ignore a red light and keep driving.
- Do not continue driving if the oil light comes on with knocking noises.

## When to get help
- Any red warning light that stays on, or a flashing engine light.
