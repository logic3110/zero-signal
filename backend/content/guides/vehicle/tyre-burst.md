## Steps
1. Grip the steering wheel firmly with both hands and keep the car pointing straight.
2. Do not slam on the brakes. Ease off the accelerator gradually.
3. If needed, apply a little accelerator briefly to stabilise the car, then let it slow down naturally.
4. Once below about 30 km/h, brake gently and steer smoothly to the side of the road.
5. Switch on hazard lights and stop on firm ground well away from traffic.
6. Get everyone out on the side away from traffic and follow the flat tyre guide or call for help.

## Warnings
- Do not brake hard or turn sharply immediately after a burst; the car can spin or roll.
- Do not drive on the burst tyre further than necessary to reach safety.

## When to get help
- Call roadside assistance if the wheel rim is damaged or it is unsafe to change the tyre.
