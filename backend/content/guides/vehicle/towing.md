## Steps
1. Find the towing eye on both vehicles; on many cars it screws into a hole in the bumper and is kept with the spare wheel.
2. Attach a rated tow rope or strap, typically no longer than about 4.5 metres, with a flag or cloth in the middle so others can see it.
3. The towed car must have the ignition on (to unlock steering), hazard lights on and a driver who can steer and brake.
4. Agree signals in advance, for example flashing lights to stop.
5. Pull away very gently to take up slack, then drive slowly, below 25 km/h, and brake early and gently.
6. Most automatic and four-wheel-drive vehicles should not be towed with wheels on the ground; check the manual.

## Warnings
- Do not attach tow ropes to suspension parts, bumpers or axles.
- Do not stand near a rope under tension.
- Brakes on the towed car need much more pressure without the engine running.
