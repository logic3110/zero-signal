## Steps
1. A silent EV may still be "on". Put it in Park, switch it off and keep the key away from the car before working near it.
2. Never touch orange high-voltage cables or components.
3. If the car was in a crash or flood, do not touch damaged parts and keep people away from the vehicle.
4. Signs of battery trouble: hissing, popping, smoke, sweet or chemical smell, or heat from under the floor. Get everyone out and far away, and call the fire service.
5. EV battery fires can reignite hours later; tell responders it is an electric vehicle.
6. When charging, use undamaged cables and approved chargers; stop charging if the connector gets hot.
7. For a flat 12-volt battery in an EV or hybrid, use only the jump points given in the owner's manual.

## Warnings
- Do not try to put out an EV battery fire yourself; it needs very large amounts of water.
- Do not tow an EV with drive wheels on the ground unless the manual allows it.
