## Steps
1. Signs of a broken drive belt: loud slap or squeal, battery light on, heavy steering, and temperature rising.
2. Pull over safely and switch off the engine. Let it cool before touching anything.
3. Look at the belt path; a snapped or shredded belt must be replaced. Carry a spare if on remote roads.
4. For a leaking coolant hose, find the leak when cool. Small splits can be wrapped tightly with self-amalgamating or duct tape as a temporary fix.
5. When the engine is cool, top up the coolant (or clean water in an emergency) and drive gently to the nearest mechanic, watching the temperature gauge.
6. Get the belt or hose replaced as soon as possible.

## Warnings
- Do not drive far without a drive belt; the engine can overheat and the battery will go flat.
- Do not touch hoses or open the cooling system while hot.
