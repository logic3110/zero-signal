## Steps
1. Stop accelerating as soon as the wheels spin; spinning digs you in deeper.
2. Straighten the front wheels.
3. Dig away mud, sand or snow from in front of and behind the driving wheels and from under the car.
4. Put traction under the driving wheels: floor mats, branches, gravel, sand, cardboard or traction boards.
5. In sand, let some air out of the tyres to increase their footprint (reinflate as soon as possible).
6. Use second gear (or the lowest gear with gentle throttle in an automatic), and accelerate very gently.
7. If needed, rock the car gently forwards and backwards to build momentum.
8. Have passengers push only from a safe position, never directly behind the spinning wheels.

## Warnings
- Do not let anyone stand behind or in line with spinning wheels; debris can be thrown out.
- Keep the exhaust pipe clear of snow if the engine is running, to avoid carbon monoxide.

## When to get help
- Use a tow from another vehicle with a proper tow strap only at rated recovery points.
