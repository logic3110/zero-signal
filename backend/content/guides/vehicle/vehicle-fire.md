## Steps
1. Pull over as soon as it is safe, stop, and switch off the engine.
2. Get everyone out immediately and move at least 30 metres away, upwind and away from traffic.
3. Call the emergency number or fire service.
4. Warn other traffic from a safe distance.
5. Only use a vehicle fire extinguisher on a small fire if you have a clear escape route, aiming through the grille or gaps without opening the bonnet.

## Warnings
- Do not open the bonnet; fresh air feeds the fire.
- Do not go back for belongings.
- Do not use water on fuel or electrical fires.

## When to get help
- Always call the fire service for a vehicle fire, even if it seems to go out.
