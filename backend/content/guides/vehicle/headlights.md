## Steps
1. Switch on hazard lights, slow down gradually and move to the side of the road using street lights or other traffic lights.
2. Try switching between dipped and main beam, and try the fog lights; one of them may still work.
3. Once stopped safely, check the headlight fuse and relay.
4. Check the bulbs: a broken filament or darkened glass means it has blown. Replace with the same type if you have a spare.
5. If only one headlight works, drive slowly on dipped beam to the nearest safe place.

## Warnings
- Do not drive at night with no headlights.
- Do not touch halogen bulb glass with bare fingers.
