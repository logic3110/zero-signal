## Steps
1. Put a sheet of white paper or cardboard under the car overnight to see the colour and position of drips.
2. Clear and watery near the front passenger side: usually air-conditioning condensation, which is normal.
3. Green, orange or pink and sweet smelling, near the front: coolant. Check the coolant level and look for hose leaks.
4. Brown or black and oily, under the engine: engine oil. Check the oil level with the dipstick.
5. Red or pink and oily, middle or front: automatic transmission or power steering fluid.
6. Clear to yellow-brown and slippery near a wheel or under the brake master cylinder: brake fluid. This is dangerous.
7. Strong petrol or diesel smell and wet patch: fuel leak. Do not start the engine.

## Warnings
- Do not drive with a brake fluid or fuel leak.
- Coolant is poisonous and tastes sweet; keep children and animals away from it.

## When to get help
- Brake fluid or fuel leaks, or any leak with a warning light, need a mechanic before driving.
