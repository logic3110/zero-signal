## Steps
1. Clutch slipping (engine revs rise but car does not speed up): drive gently, avoid hills and heavy loads, and go straight to a mechanic.
2. Clutch pedal goes to the floor and will not come back: check for a broken cable or leaking fluid under the pedal area.
3. If you cannot change gear, stop the engine, select second gear, start the engine with the clutch down (or in gear if the clutch is broken, on flat road), and drive slowly to safety.
4. Grinding when changing gear: press the clutch fully and pause briefly between gears.
5. Automatic car will not move: check the gear selector is fully in D and the handbrake is off; check the transmission fluid if you can.

## Warnings
- Do not ride the clutch or rest your foot on the pedal.
- Do not keep driving an automatic that slips or shifts roughly; it can cause major damage.

## When to get help
- Burning smell from the clutch, fluid leaks, or the car will not move in any gear.
