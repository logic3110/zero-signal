## Steps
1. Stop your vehicle safely away from the crash, switch on hazard lights and put on a reflective vest if you have one.
2. Place warning triangles or ask someone to warn approaching traffic, well back from the scene.
3. Call the emergency number: give location (use the My location tool), number of vehicles and injured people, and any hazards like fire or fuel leaks.
4. Switch off the ignition of crashed vehicles if safe, and apply their handbrakes. Do not smoke.
5. Check casualties, starting with those who are silent. Check breathing and severe bleeding first.
6. Keep casualties still if they may have neck or spinal injuries; only move them if there is fire or other immediate danger.
7. With several injured people, use START triage to decide who needs help first.
8. Keep casualties warm and reassured until help arrives.

## Warnings
- Do not remove a motorcyclist's helmet unless they are not breathing and it prevents CPR.
- Do not stand between vehicles or in the path of traffic.
- Do not give injured people food or drink.

## When to get help
- Always call emergency services for any crash with injuries or hazards.
