## Steps
1. Switch off the ignition and the faulty item.
2. Find the fuse box: usually under the dashboard, in the side of the dashboard, or in the engine bay. The lid often has a diagram.
3. Identify the fuse for the faulty item from the diagram or the owner's manual.
4. Pull the fuse out with the plastic puller from the fuse box or small pliers.
5. Hold it up to the light: a blown fuse has a broken wire inside or a dark burned look.
6. Replace it with a spare of exactly the same amperage (same number and colour).
7. Switch on and test the item.

## Warnings
- Never fit a higher-rated fuse or wrap a fuse in foil or wire; it can cause a fire.
- If the new fuse blows immediately, there is a short circuit; do not keep replacing it.

## When to get help
- A fuse keeps blowing, or you smell burning plastic.
