## Steps
1. Call the emergency number.
2. Treat any obvious cause first, especially severe bleeding.
3. Lay the person flat on their back. If there is no injury to the legs, hips or spine, you may raise the legs slightly.
4. Keep them warm: put a blanket or jacket under and over them.
5. Loosen tight clothing around the neck, chest and waist.
6. Reassure them and keep them still.
7. Check breathing, pulse and responsiveness every few minutes and note the times.
8. If they become unresponsive and stop breathing normally, start CPR.

## Warnings
- Do not give food or drink; they may need surgery and could vomit.
- Do not raise the legs if you suspect a leg, hip, spine or head injury.
- Do not leave the person alone.

## When to get help
- Shock always needs emergency medical care.

## Red flags
- Pale or grey, cold and clammy skin.
- Fast weak pulse, fast shallow breathing.
- Confusion, drowsiness, thirst, fainting.
