## Steps
1. Make sure the scene is safe and call the emergency number. Tell them how many people are hurt.
2. Ask everyone who can walk to move to a safe area. They are GREEN (minor) and are checked later.
3. Check each remaining person quickly. If not breathing, open the airway. If they still do not breathe, they are BLACK (expectant/dead); move on.
4. If breathing after the airway is opened, or breathing more than 30 times a minute, they are RED (immediate).
5. Check circulation: if there is no radial (wrist) pulse or the nail bed takes more than 2 seconds to pink up, they are RED. Control severe bleeding now.
6. Check mental status: if they cannot follow a simple command like "squeeze my hand", they are RED.
7. Everyone else who cannot walk is YELLOW (delayed).
8. Mark each person (tape, cloth or pen on the forehead) and re-check them regularly because conditions change.

## Warnings
- Do not stop to give long treatment during triage; only open airways and control major bleeding.
- Do not move people with suspected spinal injury unless needed for safety.

## When to get help
- Report numbers of RED, YELLOW, GREEN and BLACK casualties to responders when they arrive.
