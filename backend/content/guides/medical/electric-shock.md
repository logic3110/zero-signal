## Steps
1. Do not touch the person until the power is off. Switch off at the mains or unplug.
2. If you cannot switch it off, stand on dry insulating material (wood, rubber, newspapers) and push the source away with a dry wooden stick.
3. For high-voltage lines, stay at least 18 metres away and call the emergency number and the power company.
4. Once safe, check for response and breathing. Start CPR if they are not breathing normally.
5. Cool any burns under running water and cover loosely.
6. Keep them lying down and watch for shock.

## Warnings
- Do not touch the person with bare hands or anything wet or metal while the power is on.
- Do not approach fallen power lines.

## When to get help
- Everyone who has had a significant electric shock should be seen by a doctor, as heart rhythm problems and internal burns can occur.
