## Steps
1. Suspect spinal injury after falls from height, diving, road crashes, or heavy blows to the head, neck or back.
2. Call the emergency number.
3. Tell the person not to move. Kneel behind their head and hold it still with your hands on both sides, in line with the body.
4. If they are breathing, keep them in the position found and keep supporting the head.
5. If they are unresponsive and not breathing normally, open the airway (a jaw thrust is preferred if trained) and start CPR; breathing comes first.
6. If they must be moved (fire, traffic, rising water, or vomiting), roll them keeping the head, neck and body in a straight line, with helpers if possible.
7. Keep them warm and reassure them while waiting.

## Warnings
- Do not move the person unless they are in danger or you must to keep the airway open.
- Do not let them twist or bend the neck.
- Do not remove a motorcycle helmet unless it prevents breathing or CPR.

## When to get help
- Every suspected spinal injury needs emergency services.

## Red flags
- Neck or back pain after trauma, numbness, tingling, weakness or loss of movement in arms or legs.
