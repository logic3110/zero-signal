## Steps
1. Find the tooth and pick it up by the crown (the white part), not the root.
2. If dirty, rinse it briefly (about 10 seconds) in milk, saline or clean water.
3. For an adult (permanent) tooth, gently push it back into the socket the right way round and have the person bite on a cloth to hold it.
4. If you cannot replant it, store it in milk, saline or the person's saliva (inside the cheek only if they are an adult and alert).
5. Control bleeding from the socket by biting on a folded clean cloth.
6. Get to a dentist as soon as possible, ideally within 60 minutes.

## Warnings
- Do not replant a baby (milk) tooth.
- Do not scrub the root or store the tooth dry or in water for long.

## When to get help
- Any knocked-out permanent tooth, broken tooth, or jaw injury needs a dentist promptly.
