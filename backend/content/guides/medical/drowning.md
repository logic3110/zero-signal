## Steps
1. Do not become a second victim: reach with a pole or rope, or throw something that floats. Only swim to rescue if trained.
2. Once out of the water, check for response and normal breathing.
3. If not breathing normally, call the emergency number, give 5 rescue breaths, then start CPR at 30 compressions to 2 breaths.
4. If breathing, place them in the recovery position and keep them warm; remove wet clothes if you can.
5. Expect vomiting; turn them onto their side and clear the mouth.
6. Keep monitoring breathing until help arrives.

## Warnings
- Do not try to drain water from the lungs or press on the belly.
- Do not enter fast-flowing or flood water to rescue someone.

## When to get help
- Everyone rescued from drowning should be checked in hospital, even if they seem fine; breathing problems can appear hours later.

## Red flags
- Coughing, breathlessness, confusion or drowsiness after being in the water.
