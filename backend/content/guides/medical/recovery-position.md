## Steps
1. Check for danger, then check for a response and normal breathing.
2. If breathing normally, call the emergency number.
3. Kneel beside them. Place the arm nearest you at a right angle to the body.
4. Bring the far arm across the chest and hold the back of that hand against their near cheek.
5. Bend the far knee up, then pull on that knee to roll them towards you onto their side.
6. Tilt the head back slightly so the airway stays open and any vomit can drain.
7. Keep checking breathing. If it stops or becomes abnormal, roll them onto their back and start CPR.
8. For a simple faint, lay the person down and raise their legs; most recover within a minute.

## Warnings
- Do not leave an unresponsive person on their back.
- Do not give anything by mouth.

## When to get help
- Any unexplained loss of consciousness needs medical assessment.
