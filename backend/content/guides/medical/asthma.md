## Steps
1. Keep calm and reassure the person. Help them sit upright, leaning slightly forward.
2. Help them use their own reliever inhaler (usually blue) as their asthma plan or the inhaler instructions say, ideally with a spacer.
3. Encourage slow, steady breaths.
4. If there is no improvement within a few minutes, repeat the reliever inhaler as their plan directs and call the emergency number.
5. Loosen tight clothing and move them away from triggers such as smoke, dust or cold air.
6. If they become unresponsive and stop breathing normally, start CPR.

## Warnings
- Do not make the person lie down.
- Do not leave them alone.
- Do not use someone else's medicine unless directed by emergency services.

## When to get help
- The inhaler is not helping, they cannot speak in full sentences, lips turn blue, or they become exhausted or drowsy.

## Red flags
- Too breathless to talk, blue lips, exhaustion or confusion.
