## Steps
1. Stop the activity and rest the injured part.
2. Apply a cold pack or ice wrapped in cloth for 15-20 minutes, several times a day for the first 2 days.
3. Support with an elastic or crepe bandage, firm but not tight, from below to above the joint.
4. Raise the injured limb above the level of the heart when resting.
5. Check fingers or toes regularly for colour, warmth and feeling; loosen the bandage if they become cold, pale or numb.
6. When walking out is necessary, use a stick or support and tape the ankle for stability.

## Warnings
- Do not put ice directly on skin.
- Do not massage or apply heat in the first 48 hours.
- Do not keep walking on a painful ankle if you cannot take 4 steps; it may be broken.

## When to get help
- You cannot bear weight or take 4 steps, there is bone tenderness, obvious deformity, or numbness.
- Pain and swelling do not improve after a few days.
