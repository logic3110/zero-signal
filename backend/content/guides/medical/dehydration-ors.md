## Steps
1. Give oral rehydration solution (ORS) made from a packet with the exact amount of clean water stated on the packet.
2. If no packet is available, make a home solution: 1 litre of clean water, 6 level teaspoons of sugar and half a level teaspoon of salt, mixed well.
3. Give small, frequent sips from a cup or spoon. If the person vomits, wait 10 minutes and then continue more slowly.
4. Keep breastfeeding babies and continue normal food as soon as the person can eat.
5. Give zinc to children with diarrhoea if available and advised by a health worker.
6. Use a fresh batch of ORS each day and keep it covered.

## Warnings
- Do not give soft drinks, sports drinks or undiluted fruit juice as a replacement for ORS in young children.
- Do not add extra salt or sugar to the recipe.
- Do not stop breastfeeding.

## When to get help
- Signs of severe dehydration: very sleepy or unconscious, unable to drink, sunken eyes, skin pinch goes back very slowly, no urine for many hours.
- Blood in stools, high fever, or diarrhoea lasting more than a few days, especially in infants, the elderly or pregnant women.

## Red flags
- A floppy, very sleepy or unconscious child, or unable to drink.
