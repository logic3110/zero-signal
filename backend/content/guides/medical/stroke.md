## Steps
1. Check BE-FAST: Balance loss, Eyes (sudden vision loss), Face drooping, Arm weakness (cannot raise both arms), Speech slurred or strange, Time to call.
2. Call the emergency number immediately if any sign is present.
3. Note the exact time symptoms started or when the person was last seen well. Tell the medics.
4. Keep the person comfortable, lying with head and shoulders slightly raised, or in the recovery position if drowsy.
5. Loosen tight clothing and reassure them.
6. Check breathing regularly; start CPR if they stop breathing normally.

## Warnings
- Do not give food, drink or medicines by mouth; swallowing may be affected.
- Do not wait to see if symptoms improve; even brief symptoms need urgent assessment.
- Do not give aspirin for a suspected stroke.

## When to get help
- Every suspected stroke is an emergency. Get to a hospital that can treat stroke as fast as possible.

## Red flags
- Sudden face droop, arm weakness, slurred speech, loss of balance or vision, or the worst headache of their life.
