## Steps
1. For chemicals, rinse the eye immediately with gentle running water for at least 20 minutes, holding the eyelids open. Tilt the head so water runs away from the other eye.
2. Remove contact lenses if they come out easily.
3. For dust or grit, blink and rinse with clean water. You can lift the upper lid over the lower lid.
4. For an object stuck in the eye, do not remove it. Cover the eye with a paper cup or shield taped in place without pressing on it.
5. Cover both eyes loosely if possible to reduce eye movement, and reassure the person.
6. For a blow to the eye, apply a cold pack wrapped in cloth gently for 15 minutes without pressing on the eyeball.

## Warnings
- Do not rub the eye.
- Do not try to remove an object stuck in the eye.
- Do not press on an injured eye.

## When to get help
- All chemical, penetrating, or high-speed injuries, and any change in vision, need urgent eye care.
