## Steps
1. Measure the temperature if you have a thermometer. A fever is 38 degrees C or higher.
2. Dress the child in light clothing and keep the room comfortably cool.
3. Offer frequent drinks; keep breastfeeding babies.
4. Use a child fever medicine only as directed on the package or by a health worker for the child's age and weight.
5. Check the child regularly, including during the night.
6. Press a glass against any rash; if the rash does not fade under the glass, seek care immediately.

## Warnings
- Do not sponge with cold water or alcohol.
- Do not over-wrap a feverish child.
- Do not give aspirin to children.

## When to get help
- Any baby under 3 months with a fever.
- The child is very sleepy, hard to wake, floppy, has a fit, a stiff neck, a rash that does not fade, difficulty breathing, is not drinking, or has fever for more than 2-3 days.

## Red flags
- Fever in a baby under 3 months, non-fading rash, stiff neck, or unusual drowsiness.
