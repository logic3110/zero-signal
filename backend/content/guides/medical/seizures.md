## Steps
1. Stay calm and note the time the seizure started.
2. Move hard or sharp objects away and cushion the head with something soft.
3. Loosen tight clothing around the neck.
4. Let the seizure run its course; do not hold the person down.
5. When the jerking stops, roll them onto their side into the recovery position and check breathing.
6. Stay with them and talk calmly until they are fully awake; they may be confused or sleepy.
7. For a child with fever, remove excess clothing and keep them comfortable once the seizure is over.

## Warnings
- Do not put anything in their mouth, including your fingers or a spoon.
- Do not restrain their movements.
- Do not give food or drink until they are fully alert.
- Do not put a person in water or pour water on them.

## When to get help
- The seizure lasts more than 5 minutes, or a second seizure starts before they recover.
- It is their first seizure, they are injured, pregnant, diabetic, or do not wake up normally.
- The seizure happened in water, or breathing is difficult afterwards.

## Red flags
- Seizure longer than 5 minutes, repeated seizures, or not breathing normally afterwards.
